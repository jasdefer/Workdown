## In plain words

A collection bucket for small, unrelated improvements that came out of
a review — none of them urgent, none of them blocking anything.

This is not a feature in itself. It is a parent item that groups
leftover clean-ups, corner-case fixes and one small addition so they
do not float around unsorted. Each child stands on its own and can be
picked up in any order. **Example:** one child moves a piece of code to
a tidier place, another fixes how the tool handles an unusually large
number. The group deliberately has no schedule and sits off the
roadmap until one of its items becomes worth doing.
